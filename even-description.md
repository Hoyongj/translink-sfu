## Overview

The CSSS presents Fall Hacks 2022, a hackathon dedicated to learning, creativity, and making something ridiculous!

*   **Location:** SFU Burnaby. Check-in at TASC 9204 at 8:30 am (general admission). Instructions for virtual attendee check-in will be emailed ~2 days before the event.
*   **Theme:** The theme will be announced during the opening presentation.
*   **Price:** Free!
*   **Food:** Lunch and dinner will be provided to General Admission tickets. Virtual attendees will not receive food.
*   **Qualifications:** NONE! Don't have experience with programming or hacking? No worries, it's the perfect place to learn!!

## Teams

You may work individually or be part of a team. 

*   **Team Size:** Max team size is 5.
*   **Sign-up:** Teams need to be decided at signup! Teams will be specified as a part of your signup.
*   **Composition:** Non-SFU members are allowed so long as at least 50% of the team is made up of SFU students. Teams can be formed with both general and virtual members.
*   **Team Formation:** Groups can be formed using the [CSSS Discord](https://discord.gg/sfucsss) - specific team-forming channels will be opened in early October. Just make a comment asking to join a group or reply to a group asking for people to join their group! 

---

## Submissions

Submissions will be done through a link which will be emailed out to participants during the afternoon. 

*   **Requirements:** Submissions will include a list of group members, team name, project name and project link (GitHub, GitLab, other repo; or a URL to the deployed project). 
*   **Documentation:** Most importantly, submissions MUST include a `README.md` with instructions on how to build and run the submission.
*   **Rules:** Starting from an existing project that team members have worked on is against the spirit of the event. However, we highly encourage you to leverage existing open-source libraries, frameworks, or other code to get the fastest start you can!
*   **Deadline:** The submission deadline is at **19:30**! Projects submitted later will not be considered for awards.
